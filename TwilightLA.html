TWILIGHT LA
LAX TERMINAL 7
Serving as Project Lead with Baumgartner Architects, Twilight LA is a large-scale public installation at Los Angeles International Airport’s Terminal 7, integrating architecture, animation, and AI-driven media.

The project operates at the intersection of spatial design and computational media, presenting a journey through AI-generated residential environments in Los Angeles.
Domestic spaces are reimagined as sites of mystery and transformation, where the mundane becomes strange and atmospheric.

Seven distinct scenes unfold through generative video, each featuring speculative architecture and evolving landscapes created through procedural AI workflows. In the absence of human actors, the environments themselves become the protagonists—charged with ambiguity, narrative, and quiet tension.
