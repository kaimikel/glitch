TERRITORIAL MATERIALS


Territorial Materials Workshop was a research-driven workshop at SCI-Arc led by Mette Ramsgaard Thomsen and Paul Nicholas from the Royal Danish Academy, focused on alternative material sourcing through aquatic ecologies. The work investigated the use of mussels, oysters, and shell-based aggregates as components in biopolymer systems for additive manufacturing.
The project emphasized the development of sustainable material pipelines by leveraging locally sourced organic matter as both structural and textural inputs in fabrication processes.
